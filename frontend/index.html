How to use it, in four steps

About a minute from essay to report. Nib pops up at each step if you get stuck.

  1. 1Add an essay

  2. 2Add context (optional)

  3. 3Generate feedback

    The essay is analyzed against a written rubric and every quote is checked. Usually 10–40 seconds.

    How it works
    
    
    
      
    The Essay Feedback Generator produces the kind of structured, evidence-based feedback a teacher would otherwise write by hand, across the four criteria in the brief: grammar, coherence, argument strength and originality.
    
      
      
        
    1. Preprocess: normalize the text, split paragraphs and sentences, and measure sentence length and reading level.
      2. 
        
    2. Analyze: one AI call with a written 1–5 rubric, the essay type, the student's level and the assignment prompt. It returns structured JSON: scores, strengths, improvements with reasons and next steps, corrections, and argument structure.
      3. 
        
    3. Verify: every quoted sentence is matched against the essay. Comments whose quote can't be found are removed.
      4. 
        
    4. Report: the result is shown with each comment linked to the student's words, and can be printed, copied or downloaded.
      5. 
      
    
      
    Models. Free-tier Groq (gpt-oss-120b) with Google Gemini as automatic backup. 
    
      
    Honest limits. Scores are guidance on this rubric, not an official grade. Originality means fresh ideas and specific expression; it is not plagiarism or AI detection. Teachers should review feedback before grading.
    
      
    Privacy. No accounts. Drafts and recent reports stay in this browser. Essays are sent to a free AI service, so don't include personal information.
