Technology in Education

In the modern time, technology is very important for the students. Many school are using computer and tablet in classroom. I think technology help students to learn more better, but also it have some problems.

First, technology make the learning more interesting. When teacher show a video about the science, students can see how the things is working. For example in my school we watched a video of volcano and everybody was very exciting. Also students can search informations on internet very fast, they dont need to go to library.

Second, technology is helping students who is shy. Some student dont like to speak in front of class but they can write there opinion in online forum. My friend Ravi was never talking in class, but in the online discussion he write very good ideas and teacher was suprised.

However technology have also disadvantage. Students can play games or watch videos instead of study. Sometime I also do this when I am suppose to do homework. Another problem is that not every families can buy a laptop, so some students cannot use it at home. This is not fair for them.

In conclusion, technology is good for education because it make learning interesting and help shy students. But schools must to control how students use it and also give devices to poor students. If we use technology in correct way, it can make education more better for everyone.
